## 🎯 KEY BUSINESS POLICIES EMBEDDED

### Pricing & Billing
- **Free Tier**: 7-day trial, then expires (no credit card)
- **Pro Tier**: ฿299/month (auto-renew, can cancel anytime)
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing (1-year commitment)
- **Price Increases**: 30 days notice, existing customers locked in for 12 months
- **Payment Failure**: 14 days to pay before account termination
- **Late Payment**: 1.5% monthly interest + collection fees

### Refund Policy
- **Shopenter Subscriptions**: Non-refundable once billed
- **Exceptions**: Billing errors, major outages, fraud
- **Refund Window**: 14 days from charge
- **Customer Refunds**: Merchant's responsibility (not Shopenter)
- **Chargeback**: Merchant responsible for responding to bank

### Liability Protection
- **Max Liability**: Amount paid in prior 12 months
- **No Liability For**:
  - Lost profits or revenue
  - Customer disputes
  - Third-party service failures
  - Merchants' business decisions
  - Payment fraud
- **Exception**: Gross negligence, fraud, death/injury

### Trial Period
- **Duration**: 7 days free
- **Features**: Full access
- **Anti-Fraud**: One trial per person/business (violations = permanent ban)
- **No Payment Required**: Unless user upgrades

### Chargeback Protection
- **Excessive Chargebacks**: >5% triggers review, >10% = suspension
- **Chargeback Fraud**: If merchant files chargeback for items received = termination
- **Merchant Responsibility**: Merchant responds to their bank

## ⚖️ ENFORCEMENT PROCEDURES

### Level 1: Warning
- First policy violation
- Unintentional breach
- Action: Written warning email

**Template**:
```
Subject: Policy Violation - Account [ID]

Dear Merchant,

We detected a potential violation of our Acceptable Use Policy:
- Violation Type: [specific violation]
- Evidence: [details]
- Date: [when detected]

This is a warning. Please review our policies:
- AUP: https://shopenter.app/aup
- Terms: https://shopenter.app/terms

Repeated violations will result in suspension or termination.

Questions? Email abuse@shopenter.app
```

### Level 2: Suspension
- Repeated violations
- Moderate breach
- Action: Account suspended for 7-30 days

**Process**:
1. Send violation notice
2. Give 48 hours to respond/appeal
3. If appeal denied, suspend
4. During suspension, no customer access
5. Data remains; can export

### Level 3: Termination
- Severe violations
- Repeated suspensions
- Criminal activity
- Action: Permanent account deletion

**Process**:
1. Send termination notice with reason
2. 7-day appeal window
3. If appeal denied, set deletion date
4. 30-day grace period for data export
5. Permanent deletion after 30 days
6. Law enforcement referral if criminal

### Special: Immediate Termination
- No appeal or grace period:
  - Child exploitation
  - Human trafficking
  - Hacking/malware distribution
  - Organized fraud

## 🚨 CRISIS PROCEDURES

### If Merchant Violates Laws

1. **Document the violation**
   - Screenshots, dates, evidence
   - Create incident file
   - Notify legal team

2. **Investigate (24-48 hours)**
   - Verify violation is real
   - Check for mitigating factors
   - Determine severity

3. **Notify Merchant**
   - Email with details
   - Reference specific policy
   - Explain consequences
   - Provide appeal window (7 days)

4. **Report to Authorities** (if criminal)
   - Contact police/prosecutor
   - Provide evidence
   - Cooperate with investigation
   - Document cooperation

### If Data Breach Occurs

1. **Activate Incident Response** (<24 hours)
   - Identify affected data
   - Assess impact
   - Contain the breach
   - Preserve evidence

2. **Notify Merchants** (within 24 hours)
   - Email with breach details
   - Data affected
   - Exposure window
   - Remediation steps

3. **Notify Customers** (GDPR: within 72 hours)
   - Clear, honest notification
   - Include what happened
   - What we're doing
   - Resources/credit monitoring
   - Regulatory filing

4. **Post-Incident** (within 7 days)
   - Full forensic report
   - Root cause analysis
   - Prevention measures
   - Lessons learned
