# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Effective Date**: July 1, 2026  
**Last Updated**: June 28, 2026  
**Version**: 1.0

## 1. PURPOSE

This Acceptable Use Policy defines prohibited conduct on the Shopenter platform. Violations may result in account suspension or termination without refund.

---

## 2. PROHIBITED ITEMS & PRODUCTS

### 2.1 Absolutely Prohibited (Zero Tolerance)

You may NOT sell or promote:

**Illegal Goods**:
- Weapons, explosives, firearms, ammunition
- Controlled substances or drugs (including cannabis where illegal)
- Stolen or counterfeit goods
- Forged or fraudulent documents
- Products that enable criminal activity

**Health & Safety Hazards**:
- Products prohibited by Thai law
- Unlicensed medications or medical devices
- Unapproved supplements with false medical claims
- Hazardous materials without proper licensing
- Products violating consumer protection laws

**Exploitative Content**:
- Child exploitation or abuse material
- Human trafficking services
- Sexual services or exploitation
- Labor trafficking

**Intellectual Property Violations**:
- Counterfeit goods (fake designer items)
- Pirated software or media
- Unauthorized use of trademarks
- Copyright-infringing products

### 2.2 High-Risk Categories (Allowed with Verification)

These can be sold ONLY if you meet specific requirements:

**Alcohol & Tobacco**:
- Requires: Age verification, proper licensing
- Allowed: Wine, beer, spirits (with license)
- Prohibited: Sales to minors, unlicensed sales

**Prescription Medications**:
- Requires: Licensed pharmacist verification
- Requires: Government pharmacy registration
- Prohibited: Without license, shipping across borders

**Firearms & Weapons** (Limited):
- Requires: Government weapons license
- Requires: Background checks per local law
- Prohibited: To prohibited persons, without licensing

**Financial Products**:
- Requires: Financial services license
- Requires: Regulatory compliance
- Examples: Cryptocurrency, forex, penny stocks

**Gambling & Lotteries**:
- Requires: Gambling license (if legal in jurisdiction)
- Prohibited: Where illegal
- Prohibited: Operations targeting Thailand if banned

### 2.3 False & Misleading Claims

You may NOT make:
- False health claims ("cures cancer," "treats COVID")
- Unsubstantiated effectiveness claims
- Fake customer reviews or testimonials
- Misleading product descriptions
- Hidden fees or terms
- Bait-and-switch pricing
- Claims about product origin/authenticity

## 3. PROHIBITED BEHAVIORS

### 3.1 Fraud & Deception

**You may NOT**:
- Create fake accounts or shell companies
- Use false identity information
- Commit payment fraud
- Make unauthorized charges
- Use stolen payment methods
- Commit chargeback fraud
- Misrepresent business ownership

**Consequences**: Permanent ban, law enforcement referral

### 3.2 Abuse & Harassment

**You may NOT**:
- Harass, threaten, or intimidate customers
- Use hate speech or discriminatory language
- Engage in cyberbullying
- Dox or reveal private information
- Stalk or follow customers
- Spam with unwanted messages
- Use the platform for extortion

**Consequences**: Account suspension/termination

### 3.3 Intellectual Property Abuse

**You may NOT**:
- Use others' trademarks without permission
- Sell counterfeit branded goods
- Copy others' product descriptions without attribution
- Use others' images without license
- Violate copyright with your listings
- Falsely claim trademark ownership

**Consequences**: DMCA takedown, account termination

### 3.4 Platform Manipulation

**You may NOT**:
- Artificially inflate reviews through fake purchases
- Bot-create fake customer accounts
- Manipulate rating/ranking algorithms
- Make prohibited automated purchases
- Reverse-engineer the platform
- Exploit platform bugs for unfair advantage
- Automate platform access without permission

**Consequences**: Permanent ban, possible legal action

### 3.5 Data Abuse

**You may NOT**:
- Scrape or harvest customer emails
- Sell customer data to third parties
- Create unauthorized customer profiles
- Track customers off-platform without consent
- Share customer data beyond fulfillment needs
- Use customer data for unauthorized marketing

**Consequences**: Account termination, legal action

### 3.6 Account Abuse

**You may NOT**:
- Create multiple accounts to evade suspensions
- Share account login credentials
- Use accounts in someone else's name
- Operate accounts as shell companies for fraud
- Use trial period multiple times per person
- Transfer account to another person

**Consequences**: All related accounts terminated

## 10. ENFORCEMENT & CONSEQUENCES

### 10.1 Violation Levels

**Severity 1 (Warning)**:
- First-time minor violations
- Unintentional missteps
- Consequence: Written warning + education

**Severity 2 (Suspension)**:
- Repeated Severity 1 violations
- Moderate violations
- Consequence: 7-30 day suspension

**Severity 3 (Termination)**:
- Severe violations (fraud, illegal activity)
- Repeated Severity 2 violations
- Consequence: Permanent account termination

**Severity 4 (Legal Action)**:
- Criminal activity
- Major fraud
- Consequence: Termination + law enforcement referral

### 10.2 Enforcement Process

1. **Detection**: We detect violation
2. **Investigation**: We review evidence (24-48 hours)
3. **Notification**: We contact you with details
4. **Appeal Period**: 7 days to respond/appeal
5. **Decision**: We make final decision
6. **Enforcement**: Action taken
7. **Appeal**: You can appeal to management within 30 days

### 10.3 Appeal Process

**To appeal**:
- Email abuse@shopenter.app within 7 days
- Include new evidence or explanation
- Management reviews within 14 days
- Final decision issued in writing

**Do NOT**:
- Argue legal technicalities
- Attack Shopenter in public
- Create new accounts to evade ban
- Attempt social engineering
