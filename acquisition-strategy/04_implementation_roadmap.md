# ShopEnter: 90-Day Customer Acquisition Roadmap

*Prepared: May 2026 | Confidential — Internal Strategy Document*

---

## Roadmap Overview

**Start Date:** June 2, 2026 (assumed)
**End Date:** August 31, 2026
**Primary Market:** Thailand (Phase 1), Taiwan (Phase 3)
**Goal:** 50 paying customers by end of 90 days

---

## Phase 1: Foundation & Quick Wins (Week 1–2 | Jun 2–15)

### Objective
Build the minimum infrastructure for lead generation and outreach, and generate first 10–20 trial signups from warm channels.

---

### Week 1 (Jun 2–8): Infrastructure Setup

**Lead Generation Infrastructure:**

| Task | Owner | Tool | Output |
|------|-------|------|--------|
| Set up email sending domain (separate from main domain) | Tech | Instantly.ai | 3 warm-up domains (outreach@shopenter-hq.com etc.) |
| Start email warm-up (21-day process begins now) | Marketing | Instantly.ai + Mailwarm | Domains with 70+ reputation score |
| Set up Facebook Page Scraper | Marketing | Phantom Buster / Apify | 1,000+ raw leads by end of week |
| Join 5 Thai merchant Facebook Groups | Marketing | Manual | Account in groups, relationship started |
| Set up Hunter.io + NeverBounce | Marketing | Hunter + NeverBounce | Email enrichment + validation pipeline |
| Install HubSpot CRM (free tier) | Marketing/Sales | HubSpot | Deal pipeline + contact database |
| Build lead qualification scoring spreadsheet | Marketing | Google Sheets | Scoring: followers, category, estimated orders |
| Configure Google Analytics 4 + conversion tracking | Tech | GA4 | Trial signup events tracked |
| Set up Meta Ads account + payment | Marketing | Meta Business Suite | Ads account ready to launch |
| Create ShopEnter LINE OA account | Marketing | LINE | Official channel for outreach + credibility |

**Content Creation:**

| Task | Output |
|------|--------|
| Write 3 email sequence variations (Thai fashion, beauty, food) | 3 × 7-email sequences |
| Create 3 Facebook ad creatives (pain-point video, carousel, lead gen) | 3 ad sets ready |
| Build Thai-language landing page for ad traffic | /th landing page with free trial CTA |
| Create "LINE OA Order Management Checklist" (lead magnet PDF) | Downloadable guide for email sequences |

**Expected Week 1 Deliverables:**
- CRM setup and operational
- 1,000+ raw leads in database
- Email sequences drafted and in review
- All tools configured and connected
