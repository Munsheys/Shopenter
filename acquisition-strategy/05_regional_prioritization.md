# ShopEnter: Regional Prioritization Framework

*Prepared: May 2026 | Confidential — Internal Strategy Document*

---

## Regional Priority Rankings

### Priority Score Methodology

Each market scored on 6 dimensions (0–10 each):
1. **Merchant Density** — How many LINE OA merchants exist
2. **Purchasing Power** — Willingness and ability to pay for SaaS
3. **LINE OA Dependency** — How central LINE OA is to their business (harder to replace = more valuable)
4. **Competition Level** — How saturated the solution market is (higher = lower score)
5. **CAC Efficiency** — How cost-effective is customer acquisition there
6. **Language/Ops Ease** — How easy for ShopEnter to operate in this market

## Market Rankings

### Rank 1: Thailand 🇹🇭

**Priority Score: 9.2/10**

| Dimension | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Merchant Density | 10/10 | 2.7M+ active LINE OA merchants; LINE penetration >80% of population |
| Purchasing Power | 6/10 | SaaS-friendly; $30–$60/mo sweet spot for SMBs |
| LINE OA Dependency | 10/10 | LINE is THE commerce channel for Thai SMBs |
| Competition Level | 7/10 | Some competitors (Sellsuki, Zwiz) but none full-stack |
| CAC Efficiency | 9/10 | Facebook ads cheap ($1–$3 CPM); large merchant communities; Thai content resonates |
| Language/Ops Ease | 7/10 | Thai script is complex; need native speakers |
| **Total** | **9.2/10** | |

**Why Thailand First:**
- Largest addressable market in the LINE OA ecosystem
- Merchants are highly community-oriented (Facebook Groups, LINE Groups, Pantip) = easy reach
- LINE OA is existential to Thai merchant operations — not a nice-to-have
- No full-stack competitor
- Very low paid advertising costs
- Strong influencer culture for product adoption

**Thailand-Specific Strategy:**
- **Channels:** Facebook Groups (manual) → Facebook Ads → LINE Ads → Email → Webinar (Thai-language)
- **Messaging:** Focus on "ออเดอร์หายในแชท" (lost orders in chat) — this pain is universal and emotional
- **Pricing:** ฿900–฿2,500/month ($25–$70 USD) — local pricing in THB builds trust
- **Key Partnerships:**
  - Krungthai Bank / SCB KBank (largest Thai banks with SMB merchant programs)
  - Kerry Express, Flash Express (largest Thai logistics — every LINE merchant uses them)
  - Thailand e-commerce influencers on Facebook/TikTok with 100K–1M merchant followers
  - Digital marketing agencies in Bangkok (50+ agencies serve LINE OA merchants)
- **Content:** Must be 100% in Thai; Thai-style imagery (product flatlay aesthetic common in Thai market)
- **Localization required:** Thai language UI, PromptPay QR code integration, Thai tax invoices

### Rank 2: Taiwan 🇹🇼

**Priority Score: 8.6/10**

| Dimension | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Merchant Density | 8/10 | 686K+ active LINE OA merchants; 21M user base with very high LINE penetration |
| Purchasing Power | 8/10 | GDP per capita ~$35K; SMBs pay $50–$100/mo comfortably |
| LINE OA Dependency | 8/10 | LINE is dominant (WhatsApp/Facebook Messenger not primary) |
| Competition Level | 8/10 | Very low competition; Crescendo Lab is enterprise-only |
| CAC Efficiency | 8/10 | Smaller market but concentrated; Facebook ads efficient |
| Language/Ops Ease | 7/10 | Traditional Chinese required; ops-friendly (formal economy) |
| **Total** | **8.6/10** | |

**Why Taiwan Second:**
- Very high purchasing power relative to competitor density (massive opportunity gap)
- LINE penetration is near-identical to Thailand
- English sometimes acceptable in B2B context (reduces localization cost)
- Traditional Chinese content serves both Taiwan and some Hong Kong audiences
- Regulatory environment is stable and predictable

**Taiwan-Specific Strategy:**
- **Channels:** Facebook Ads (TW) → Email → LINE Ads → LinkedIn (for B2B merchants) → SEO (Traditional Chinese)
- **Messaging:** Emphasize professionalism and efficiency — Taiwanese merchants are more process-oriented than Thai
- **Pricing:** NT$800–$2,500/month ($25–$80 USD)
- **Key Partnerships:**
  - Shopline (largest TW e-commerce platform — potential integration partner)
  - 91APP (TW omnichannel platform)
  - PChome, Ruten (TW marketplaces — merchants often cross-list)
  - 104 Job Bank (job-posting intent signal monitoring)
- **Content:** Traditional Chinese UI essential; content can be more formal/business-like tone
- **Localization required:** Traditional Chinese UI, Taiwan invoice (二聯式/三聯式發票), LINE Pay integration

### Rank 3: Japan 🇯🇵

**Priority Score: 8.1/10**

| Dimension | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Merchant Density | 8/10 | 2.09M+ active LINE OA merchants; 96M users |
| Purchasing Power | 10/10 | Highest purchasing power in Asia; pay $50–$150/mo for good SaaS |
| LINE OA Dependency | 7/10 | LINE very popular; some compete with Instagram DM, Rakuten |
| Competition Level | 6/10 | More local solutions (Lステップ, エルメ); more sophisticated market |
| CAC Efficiency | 6/10 | Higher ad costs; longer sales cycle; language barrier high |
| Language/Ops Ease | 4/10 | Japanese is complex; requires native-language content + support |
| **Total** | **8.1/10** | |

**Why Japan Third:**
- Highest LTV potential (willingness to pay is highest)
- Large merchant base
- However: requires full Japanese localization, Japanese support staff, and Japanese billing/compliance
- Competition from native solutions like Lステップ (L-Step) for LINE automation
- Japanese buyers need trust-building (slower sales cycle)

**Japan-Specific Strategy:**
- **Channels:** Google Search Ads (JP keywords) → LINE Ads → Content/SEO (Japanese) → LinkedIn (JP corporate) → Partner through Japanese agencies
- **Messaging:** Quality, reliability, and professional polish are paramount in Japan; ROI proof required
- **Pricing:** ¥3,000–¥12,000/month ($20–$80 USD) — Japanese SaaS pricing conventions
- **Key Partnerships:**
  - Lステップ (LINE automation tool) — potential integration (not direct competition for merchant operations)
  - BASE, Stores.jp (Japanese e-commerce platforms)
  - Japan Post / Yamato Transport (logistics integrations)
  - LINE Business Partners program (Japan-specific)
- **Content:** Full Japanese translation required; Japanese UX standards are high
- **Localization required:** Japanese UI, Japanese invoice format, Japanese consumption tax (JCT) support
- **Entry timing:** Launch Japan in Month 4–6 after Thailand/Taiwan prove the model

### Rank 4: Singapore 🇸🇬

**Priority Score: 6.8/10**

| Dimension | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Merchant Density | 5/10 | Only 82,500 active LINE OA merchants — small but concentrated |
| Purchasing Power | 10/10 | Highest income per capita in SE Asia; $60–$150/mo easy |
| LINE OA Dependency | 5/10 | LINE is used but WhatsApp is dominant; less LINE-native |
| Competition Level | 7/10 | Low competition for LINE-specific tools |
| CAC Efficiency | 6/10 | Small market; higher ad costs; English is fine |
| Language/Ops Ease | 10/10 | English-first market; simple regulatory environment |
| **Total** | **6.8/10** | |

**Why Singapore Fourth:**
- Small market but highest revenue per customer potential
- English-language means zero localization cost
- Gateway to broader SE Asia enterprise deals
- Strong startup/SaaS culture — will be early adopter market
- WhatsApp dominance means some merchants won't be primary LINE OA users

**Singapore-Specific Strategy:**
- **Channels:** LinkedIn Ads (Singapore business owners) → Google Search → Cold email (English) → Startup event sponsorship
- **Messaging:** Efficiency, integration, analytics — Singapore buyers are data-driven and ROI-focused
- **Pricing:** SGD $40–$120/month ($30–$90 USD) — premium tier acceptable
- **Use Case:** Position Singapore as the English-language beachhead for multinational merchants operating in Thailand/Taiwan
