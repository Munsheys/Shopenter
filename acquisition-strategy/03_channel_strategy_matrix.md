*Prepared: May 2026 | Confidential — Internal Strategy Document*

## Channel Overview

This matrix evaluates all customer acquisition channels across six dimensions:
- **Automation Level:** How much can be automated vs. manual
- **Estimated CAC:** Blended customer acquisition cost (lead → paying customer)
- **Timeline to Revenue:** Weeks until first paying customers from this channel
- **Resource Requirements:** Team, budget, time
- **Priority Rank:** Launch sequencing recommendation

**Conversion rate assumptions:**
- Cold email: 0.5–2% lead → trial; 20–35% trial → paid
- Paid ads: 1.5–4% click → trial; 25–40% trial → paid
- Community/word-of-mouth: 15–30% referral → trial; 40–60% trial → paid
- SEO organic: 3–8% visit → trial; 25–40% trial → paid

## Detailed Channel Analysis

### Channel 1: Cold Email Outreach ⭐ Priority 1

**Tool Recommendations:**

| Tool | Best For | Cost | Deliverability | Automation |
|------|----------|------|----------------|-----------|
| **Instantly.ai** | High-volume cold outreach | $37–$97/mo | Excellent | Very High |
| **Lemlist** | Personalized + LinkedIn combo | $59–$99/mo | Good | High |
| **Smartlead.ai** | Warm-up + multi-sender | $39–$94/mo | Excellent | Very High |
| **HubSpot (Starter)** | CRM-integrated sequences | $20–$90/mo | Good | Medium |

**Recommended:** Instantly.ai for volume + HubSpot Starter for CRM tracking.

**Email Sequence Structure (7-touch, 21 days):**

| Day | Type | Subject Line Angle | CTA |
|-----|------|-------------------|-----|
| 1 | Cold intro | "How [merchant name] is losing orders in LINE chat" | Free trial link |
| 3 | Pain point | "Your competitors are saving 3hrs/day — here's how" | Case study |
| 7 | Social proof | "100+ LINE OA merchants in [city] switched to this" | Demo video |
| 10 | Value delivery | Free guide: "LINE OA Order Management Checklist" | Download |
| 14 | Objection handle | "Takes 10 min to set up, no credit card needed" | Free trial |
| 18 | Urgency | "Offer ends soon: 3 months free for early adopters" | Signup |
| 21 | Breakup | "Should I stop reaching out?" | Reply = re-engage |

**Personalization Variables:**
- Business name (LINE OA account name from directory)
- Category (fashion/food/beauty — different pain points)
- Follower count bracket (micro vs. small)
- Region (Thai copy vs. English copy)
- Seasonal hook (holidays, sales seasons)

**Expected Metrics:**
- Open rate: 35–55% (with domain warm-up + Thai-language subject lines)
- Reply rate: 5–12%
- Trial rate: 2–5% of emails sent
- Trial → paid: 25–35%
- CAC: $35–$75

### Channel 2: Facebook/Instagram Ads ⭐ Priority 1

**Audience Targeting (Thailand):**
- Interest: LINE Official Account, online selling, e-commerce, ขายของออนไลน์
- Job title: Business owner, Store owner, Shop owner, Merchant
- Behavior: Small business owners, Facebook Page admins (business pages)
- Lookalike: 1–3% lookalike from trial signups (after 500+ trials)
- Exclude: Employees at large companies, job seekers

**Ad Formats by Funnel Stage:**

| Stage | Format | Message | Budget % |
|-------|--------|---------|----------|
| Awareness | Video (15s) | Pain point demo: "ออเดอร์หายในแชท LINE?" | 30% |
| Consideration | Carousel | Feature benefits: order tracking, customer DB, website | 40% |
| Conversion | Lead Gen Form | Free trial offer, no credit card | 20% |
| Retargeting | Single Image | Social proof + urgency | 10% |

**Expected Metrics (Thailand):**
- CPM: $2–$5 (Thai audience cheap)
- CPC: $0.15–$0.50
- CTR: 1.5–3.5% (localized creative)
- Trial conversion: 3–6%
- CAC: $40–$90

**Creative Strategy:**
- Video ads showing before/after: spreadsheet chaos vs. ShopEnter dashboard
- Thai-language copy is essential — English ads get 40–60% lower CTR
- UGC-style: real merchant testimonials (get 3–5 beta users on video)
- Mobile-first: 9:16 vertical video for Stories/Reels

### Channel 6: LINE Ads Platform ⭐ Priority 2

**Why This Is Underrated:**
LINE Ads Platform allows targeting users based on LINE usage behavior, profile data, and interest categories. For a LINE-native product, this is unmatched relevance.

**Targeting Options:**
- LINE OA followers of competitor accounts (if available)
- Age 25–45 + Business owner + Thailand
- Interest: Business tools, e-commerce
- Retargeting: Website visitors who viewed pricing page

**Ad Formats:**
- LINE Smart Channel ads (card-style in LINE chat list)
- LINE Timeline sponsored posts
- LINE OA messaging ads (requires existing OA relationship)

**Cost Benchmarks:**
- CPM: $3–$8
- CPC: $0.30–$0.80
- CTR: 0.8–2% (LINE users are less ad-accustomed)
- CAC: $40–$90

### Channel 15: Webinar/Live Commerce ⭐ Priority 2

**Thailand Facebook Live Culture:**
Thai merchants heavily use Facebook Live for product launches and announcements. ShopEnter can leverage this with:
- Monthly "LINE OA Merchant Masterclass" (free webinar)
- Topics: "Double your LINE OA sales in 30 days," "LINE OA order management secrets"
- Registration → email capture → nurture sequence → trial offer
- Partner with Thai business coaches/influencers as co-hosts

**Estimated Results per Webinar:**
- Registrations: 200–800 (with paid promotion)
- Attendance rate: 30–50%
- Trial signup: 15–25% of attendees
- Paid conversion: 30–40% of trials

**Cost per Webinar:** $300–$800 (Zoom/Airmeet + promotion)
**CAC via Webinar:** $20–$50 (excellent)
