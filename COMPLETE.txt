  ├─ Marketing site with features and benefits
  ├─ Shows what your service does
  ├─ Has "Get Started Free" and "Sign In" buttons
  └─ Professional design with stats and testimonials

  /signup (Sign-Up)
  ├─ Create merchant account
  ├─ Fields: Shop Name, Email, Password
  ├─ Creates account and redirects to dashboard
  └─ Option to login if already have account

  /login (Sign-In)
  ├─ Sign in with email and password
  ├─ Redirects to dashboard on success

  └─ Storefront management options

  ├─ Customer-facing shop
  ├─ No authentication required
  ├─ Unique URL for each merchant
  ├─ Shows merchant's branding and products
  ├─ Shopping cart and checkout
  ├─ PromptPay QR payment
  └─ Orders created here appear in merchant's dashboard

  Merchant A can ONLY see:
    ✓ Their own products
    ✓ Their own orders
    ✓ Their own customers
    ✓ Their own settings

  Merchant A CANNOT see:
    ✗ Merchant B's products
    ✗ Merchant B's orders
    ✗ Merchant B's customers
    ✗ Merchant B's data

  WHY: Every database query includes merchantId filter
       JWT contains merchantId from login
       Server validates on every request
