# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Effective Date**: July 1, 2026  
**Applies To**: GDPR, Thai PDPA, CCPA Compliance
**Version**: 1.0

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION & DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Purpose
This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs how Shopenter processes personal data on behalf of merchants under GDPR, Thai PDPA, CCPA, and other data protection laws.

### 1.2 Definitions

**Data Controller**: The merchant (you) - decides what data to collect and why

**Data Processor**: Shopenter Limited - processes data on your behalf, per your instructions

**Personal Data**: Any information identifying or relating to an individual

**Processing**: Any operation on personal data (collection, storage, use, deletion)

**Sub-processor**: Third-party vendors who access your data

**Data Subject**: Individual whose data is processed (your customers)

---

## 2. SCOPE & APPLICABILITY

### 2.1 When DPA Applies
This DPA applies if:
- You collect data from individuals in EU (GDPR)
- You collect data from individuals in Thailand (Thai PDPA)
- You collect data from individuals in California (CCPA)
- You collect any "personal data" under applicable laws

### 2.2 What Data It Covers
Personal data processed by Shopenter on your behalf:
- Customer names, emails, phone numbers
- Delivery addresses
- Order history
- Payment confirmations (not full payment details)
- Customer preferences
- Device identifiers
- IP addresses (customer)

### 2.3 What It Doesn't Cover
- Your internal business data (not personal data)
- Aggregated/anonymized data
- Public data
- Non-personal business information

## 3. PARTIES & ROLES

### 3.1 You are the Data Controller
As merchant, you:
- Decide what customer data to collect
- Determine purposes (e.g., order fulfillment)
- Set retention periods
- Handle customer rights requests
- Ensure legal basis for processing
- Comply with privacy laws

### 3.2 Shopenter is the Data Processor
As processor, we:
- Process data only per your instructions
- Cannot use data for our own purposes
- Must protect data security
- Comply with your data protection requests
- Provide technical and organizational measures
- Cooperate with authorities

### 3.3 Responsibility Allocation

| Area | Merchant (Controller) | Shopenter (Processor) |
|------|---|---|
| Legal basis for processing | ✓ Your responsibility | Our responsibility to follow instructions |
| Privacy notices to customers | ✓ Your responsibility | Our responsibility to enable |
| Customer rights requests | ✓ Yours to manage | Ours to facilitate |
| Data security | Shared | Our responsibility for our systems |
| Breach notification | ✓ Yours to customer | Ours to you |
| Legal compliance | Primarily yours | Shared for processor obligations |

## 4. DATA PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS

### 4.1 Permitted Processing
Shopenter processes customer data to:
- Store customer information
- Manage orders (fulfillment)
- Send order notifications
- Display customer info for merchant
- Provide analytics and reporting
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

### 4.2 Prohibited Processing
Shopenter will NOT:
- Use customer data for our marketing
- Build profiles about customers
- Sell or share customer data
- Use data for credit scoring
- Engage in discriminatory profiling
- Use for automated decision-making (without consent)

### 4.3 Modification of Instructions
- You may modify processing instructions
- Changes effective after 30 days notice
- Shopenter complies with new instructions
- If new instructions violate law, we notify you
- We may terminate if instructions become illegal

## 7. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

### 7.1 We Facilitate These Rights
Shopenter helps you respond to:

**Right to Access**:
- Data subjects can request their data
- Merchant responds (we provide data)
- Shopenter exports on request
- Response within 14 days

**Right to Rectification**:
- Correct inaccurate data
- We provide tools to update
- We document corrections
- Audit trail maintained

**Right to Erasure**:
- Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- We provide deletion tools
- We delete within 30 days (unless legal hold)
- Audit logs kept separately

**Right to Restrict Processing**:
- Limit how data is used
- We implement restrictions
- We limit access/processing
- We log restrictions

**Right to Data Portability**:
- Export data in structured format
- We provide CSV/JSON export
- Within 30 days of request
- We facilitate merchant compliance

**Right to Object**:
- Opt-out of certain processing
- We unsubscribe from marketing
- We implement restrictions
- We honor choices

**Rights Related to Automated Decisions**:
- Right not to be profiled
- We provide manual review option
- We explain automated decisions
- We provide human contact

### 7.2 Our Obligations
When data subject rights requested:
- We notify merchant immediately
- We provide tools/data to comply
- We extend our deadlines if needed for merchant compliance
- We track compliance documentation

### 7.3 Timing
- Standard requests: 14-30 days
- Complex requests: up to 60 days
- Urgent requests: 5-7 days
- We prioritize legitimate requests

## 11. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

### 11.1 Retention by Shopenter
- Active account: Data retained as long as needed
- Deleted account: 30-day export window
- After 30 days: Automatic deletion
- Audit logs: 7 years (legal requirement)
- Backups: 30 days (disaster recovery)

### 11.2 Deletion Process
1. Merchant requests deletion
2. We mark for deletion (grace period)
3. After 30 days: Automatic permanent deletion
4. Recovery impossible after deletion
5. Confirmation email sent

### 11.3 Retained Data
We retain after deletion:
- Audit logs (7 years required by law)
- Backup copies (30 days for recovery)
- Anonymized analytics (indefinite)
- Aggregated statistics (indefinite)

### 11.4 Legal Holds
We retain if:
- Requested by court order
- Fraud investigation ongoing
- Dispute being resolved
- Payment obligations outstanding
- Legal obligation applies
