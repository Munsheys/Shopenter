## 8. DATA SECURITY

### 8.1 Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption in transit**: TLS/HTTPS (all data encrypted)
- **Encryption at rest**: AES-256-CBC for secrets
- **Password security**: bcrypt with 12 rounds (industry standard)
- **Authentication**: JWT tokens with 7-day expiry
- **CSRF protection**: Tokens on all state-changing requests
- **Rate limiting**: Prevents brute force attacks
- **Audit logging**: Tracks all sensitive operations

### 8.2 Administrative Safeguards
- Access controls (role-based)
- Employee training on data protection
- Non-disclosure agreements with staff
- Regular security audits
- Incident response procedures
- Vulnerability testing
- Penetration testing (annually)

### 8.3 Physical Safeguards
- Data centers with 24/7 security
- Redundant systems (geographic backup)
- Fire suppression systems
- Power backup systems
- CCTV monitoring
- Limited physical access

### 8.4 What We Cannot Guarantee
- 100% security (impossible)
- Zero vulnerabilities
- Prevention of all data breaches
- Protection against nation-state attacks
